# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Beatmakers, game audio hobbyists, synth-curious musicians, streamers, and developers who want to sketch loops and trigger performance samples quickly in the browser without opening a full DAW.

## Product Purpose
Music Man is a browser sequencer and soundboard for building hard-edged loops with immediate playback, pattern generation, track-level shaping, performance pads, and shareable/savable ideas. Success means users can land on the app, start audio, shape a groove, fire one-shots or vocal callouts, mutate it, and keep the result without needing music theory or setup.

## Brand Personality
Electric, precise, playful. The interface should feel like a compact performance instrument: dense enough for repeated use, expressive enough to make happy accidents, and clear enough that the grid remains the main event.

## Anti-references
Avoid tutorial-first toys, desktop-only experiences, bland gray DAW clones, fake retro skeuomorphism, oversized marketing pages, and visual decoration that competes with the beat grid.

## Design Principles
- Make sound editable at the same speed as thought.
- Keep the rhythm grid visually dominant and readable.
- Let generation and mutation create musical starting points, not random noise.
- Expose power through compact controls instead of modal-heavy workflows.
- Use motion as musical feedback: playhead, meters, active states.
- Treat sample clips as first-class performance material: bundled owned kits, user-loaded audio, and replaceable sample slots should be obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable playback and cell toggles, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, readable labels for icon buttons, and color-plus-shape state cues so active steps do not rely only on hue.
